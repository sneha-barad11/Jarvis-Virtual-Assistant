*Tips for customizing the letter:*

1. **Insert accurate dates** – Ensure the start and end dates reflect the period recommended by your physician.  
2. **Adjust tone** – Use a formal tone for corporate settings; a slightly informal tone may be appropriate for small businesses or startups.  
3. **Attach supporting documents** – If your organization requires a medical certificate, mention that you will attach it.  
4. **Follow company policy** – Some firms have specific formats or portals for leave applications; adapt the content accordingly.
