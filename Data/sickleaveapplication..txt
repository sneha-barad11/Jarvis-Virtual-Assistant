I am writing to inform you that, unfortunately, I have fallen ill and will be unable to attend work for [number] days. I am currently under medical treatment and my doctor has advised me to take complete rest for a speedy recovery.

I am requesting sick leave from [start date] to [end date]. I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and will make sure to catch up on any missed work as soon as I return to the office.

I have attached a medical certificate from my doctor, which confirms my illness and the required period of leave. If there's any additional documentation required, please let me know.

I would appreciate it if you could approve my sick leave application at the earliest. If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Thank you for your understanding and support during this time.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Name]
