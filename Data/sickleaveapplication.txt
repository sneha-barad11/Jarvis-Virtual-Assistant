Here is another sample Sick Leave Application:

To,
[Supervisor's Name]
[Supervisor's Designation]
[Company Name]
[Company Address]

Date: [Current Date]

Subject: Application for Sick Leave

Respected [Supervisor's Name],

I am writing to inform you that I am suffering from [illness/health issue] and am currently under medical treatment. Due to this, I will be unable to attend work from [start date] to [end date].

I have consulted my doctor and am undergoing treatment as per their advice. I am attaching a medical certificate from my doctor, which confirms my illness and recommends the necessary leave.

I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and will make sure to catch up on any missed work as soon as I am able to return to the office. I will ensure that all my tasks are completed as soon as possible and will be available by email if anything urgent comes up during my absence.

I would like to request [number] days of sick leave, from [start date] to [end date]. I will provide any additional documentation or information required by the company to support my application.

Please let me know if there are any issues or if there is any additional information you need from me. I appreciate your understanding and support during this time.

Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Your Employee ID]
[Your Designation]

Enclosure: Medical Certificate

Note: Please modify the application as per your company's policies and requirements. Also, make sure to attach a medical certificate from your doctor to support your application.
