**Leave Application – Sample Formats**

Below are three ready‑to‑use templates that you can adapt instantly for different situations (medical, personal, academic, or professional).  
Simply replace the placeholders (in **bold**) with your own details.

---

## 1️⃣ Formal Leave Application – Office / Workplace  

**[Your Name]**  
**[Your Designation]**  
**[Department]**  
**[Company/Organization Name]**  
**[Company Address]**  

**Date:** *[DD Month YYYY]*  

**To**  
**The Manager**  
**[Department/Division]**  
**[Company/Organization Name]**  

**Subject:** *Application for Leave from [Start Date] to [End Date]*  

> Dear Sir/Madam,  

I am writing to request **[type of leave – e.g., casual / sick / earned]** leave for the period **[Start Date]** to **[End Date]** (total **[X]** working days).  

**Reason (briefly):**  
- *[Medical certificate attached / Family function / Personal emergency / Vacation]*  

I have ensured that my current tasks are either completed or handed over to **[Colleague’s Name]**, who will cover any urgent matters in my absence. All pending files are updated in the shared drive, and I will remain reachable via **[phone/email]** for any critical issues.

I kindly request you to approve my leave. Thank you for your understanding.

> **Thank you.**  

> **Yours sincerely,**  
> **[Your Signature (if printed)]**  
> **[Your Name]**  
> **Employee ID:** *[if applicable]*  

---

## 2️⃣ Student Leave Application – School / College  

**To**  
**The Principal / Head of Department**  
**[School/College Name]**  
**[Address]**  

**Date:** *[DD Month YYYY]*  

**Subject:** *Application for Leave from [Start Date] to [End Date]*  

> Respected Sir/Madam,  

I, **[Your Full Name]**, a student of **[Class/Year & Section]**, wish to seek leave for **[number]** days, from **[Start Date]** to **[End Date]**, due to **[reason – e.g., fever, family function, medical treatment]**.  

I have attached a medical certificate/parent’s note for your reference. I will catch up on all missed lessons and assignments promptly upon my return.

I request you to kindly grant me leave.  

> **Thank you.**  

> **Yours faithfully,**  
> **[Your Signature]**  
> **[Roll No./Student ID]**  

---

## 3️⃣ Academic/Research Leave Application – University (Faculty or PhD Scholar)  

**From:**  
**Dr./Mr./Ms. [Your Name]**  
**[Designation – e.g., Assistant Professor / PhD Candidate]**  
**[Department]**  
**[University Name]**  

**To:**  
**The Dean / Head of Department**  
**[University Name]**  

**Date:** *[DD Month YYYY]*  

**Subject:** *Application for Leave (Research/Medical/Personal) – *[Start Date] to [End Date]*  

> Dear Prof. [Dean/Head’s Last Name],  

I am writing to request a **[type]** leave of **[X]** days, from **[Start Date]** to **[End Date]**, due to **[brief reason – e.g., a scheduled medical procedure, participation in a conference, personal emergency]**.  

During my absence, I have arranged for **[Colleague/Co‑author’s Name]** to supervise the ongoing experiments/lectures and will be reachable via **[email/phone]** for any urgent queries. All required documentation (medical certificate, conference invitation, etc.) is attached.

I would be grateful for your approval.

> **Thank you for your consideration.**  

> **Sincerely,**  
> **[Your Signature]**  
> **[Your Name]**  
> **Employee/Staff ID:** *[if applicable]*  

---

### Quick Checklist Before Sending

| ✔️ | Item | Why |
|---|------|-----|
|   | **Correct dates** – double‑check start & end dates. |
|   | **Reason clarity** – keep it concise but specific enough for verification. |
|   | **Attachments** – medical certificate, event invitation, parental note, etc. |
|   | **Work handover plan** – mention colleague or supervisor covering you. |
|   | **Contact details** – phone/email for emergencies. |
|   | **Polite closing** – thank the reader and sign off properly. |

Feel free to copy any of the above templates, fill in your particulars, and send it via email, printed letter, or your institution’s online portal. Good luck, and I hope your leave gets approved smoothly! 🚀
