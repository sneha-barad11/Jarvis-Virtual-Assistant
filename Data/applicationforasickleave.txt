[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, Pincode]
[Date]

[Recipient's Name]
[Recipient's Designation]
[Company/Organization Name]
[Company/Organization Address]
[City, State, Pincode]

Subject: Application for Sick Leave

Dear [Recipient's Name],

I am writing to inform you that I will be unable to attend work from [start date] to [end date] due to health reasons. I am currently suffering from [mention the illness or medical condition] and am under medical treatment.

I have attached a medical certificate from my doctor, which confirms my illness and recommends rest for the aforementioned period. I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and will make sure to catch up on any missed work as soon as I am able to return to the office.

I would like to request sick leave for [number of days] days, from [start date] to [end date]. I will ensure that all my tasks are completed as soon as possible and will be available by email if anything urgent comes up during my absence.

Please let me know if there are any issues or if there is any additional information you need from me. I appreciate your understanding and support during this time.

Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Name]

Attachments: Medical Certificate

Note: Please modify the application to fit your specific needs and circumstances. Also, make sure to keep a copy of the application for your records.
