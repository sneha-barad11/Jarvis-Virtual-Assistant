**Title: “Echoes of Tomorrow”**

*Genre: Acoustic‑pop with a gentle, uplifting vibe*  

---

**[Intro – soft guitar picking]**  
(Instrumental, 8 bars)

---

**[Verse 1]**  
When sunrise paints the sky in gold,  
I hear a whisper in the wind,  
A story that the day unfolds,  
A brand‑new chapter set to begin.  

Footsteps echo on the pavement,  
Dreams are humming in the air,  
Every heartbeat is a promise,  
Every breath a silent prayer.

---

**[Pre‑Chorus]**  
And if the world feels heavy,  
Just lift your eyes and see—  
The rhythm of the universe,  
Beating in sync with you and me.

---

**[Chorus]**  
*We’re the echo of tomorrow,  
A melody that never fades,  
Through the storm and bright tomorrow,  
We’ll find the light inside the shades.*  

*Sing it loud, sing it clear,  
Let the chorus rise and steer,  
All our hopes, our doubts, our fears—*  
*Turn to notes that we re‑hear.*

---

**[Verse 2]**  
The city hums a lullaby,  
Neon lights become our stars,  
We chase the moments passing by,  
Collecting wonder from afar.  

A laugh, a tear, a fleeting glance,  
They’re verses in our song,  
Together we’ll create the dance,  
Where every soul belongs.

---

**[Pre‑Chorus]**  
When shadows start to linger,  
Just trust the rhythm’s flow—  
The music’s made of strangers,  
Yet it’s yours, it’s mine, it’s ours to know.

---

**[Chorus]** *(repeat)*  
*We’re the echo…*  

---

**[Bridge – stripped‑down piano & strings]**  
Let the silence speak in pauses,  
Every pause a space to breathe,  
In the quiet, find the cause—  
A gentle strength we both can weave.  

*Take my hand, we’ll write the line,  
A chorus that will never die,  
Your voice, my voice, entwined—*  
*Together, we’ll touch the sky.*

**[Final Chorus – full band, soaring vocals]**  
*We’re the echo of tomorrow,
